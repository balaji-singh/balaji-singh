## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm **Bala**, based in Vienna, Austria, working at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and security:

- 🛡️ **Tech Lead & CISO at OSL Austria (OSL EU)** — a Crypto-Asset Service Provider regulated under **MiCAR** and **DORA**. I own technical incident response and FMA regulatory notification, and lead the firm's DORA ICT risk and compliance program.
- ☁️ **Senior Cloud Engineer**, focused on **Service Mesh** and **Cloud Networking** — designing resilient, secure platforms that hold up under regulatory scrutiny as well as production load.
- 🐼 Creator of **The Cloud Panda** — a YouTube channel and toolset at [thecloudpanda.com](https://thecloudpanda.com) where I share hands-on cloud engineering content.

- 🔭 Currently building **DORA ICT compliance tooling** and a **KEDA-style event-driven autoscaler** (Python)
- 🌱 Currently deepening **service mesh architecture, Kubernetes internals, and MLOps**
- 💬 Ask me about **AWS, Kubernetes, service mesh, cloud security, DORA/MiCAR compliance, or IaC**
